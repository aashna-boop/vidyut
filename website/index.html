Why Vidyut

Solar is outrunning the wires. Vidyut forecasts where the grid will choke, finds a fault the moment it appears and reroutes power around it before a crew is dispatched.

That's the ambition. What exists today is a prototype: real grid models, real hourly weather and demand, real power-flow physics — and no field data. Everything on this page says which is which.

Integration layer

Speaks
61850

Vidyut reads breaker state and measurements, publishes forecasts and localizations, and issues switching commands with select-before-operate — using IEC 61850-style tags, plus a DNP3-style point map for legacy RTUs.

Tag names follow 61850 conventions; they must be checked against a utility's SCL files before real use.
