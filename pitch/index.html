09 · What's real, what isn't

20×the 90 days of the first prototype
0real utility incidents — all faults injected
Real

4 published grids (15–294 buses), AC power flow every hour, recorded weather and demand.

Injected

Faults, theft, curtailment labels. Grids are German benchmarks, not an Indian feeder.

Verdict

Rigorous proof-of-concept on real physics. Not yet a live Indian grid.
